TWO MAGIC PARAMETERS:

1️⃣  'save_period': 1
    ├─ Saves checkpoint after EVERY epoch
    ├─ Filename: epoch1.pt, epoch2.pt, ..., epoch150.pt
    ├─ Storage: 650 MB × 150 = 97.5 GB for yolo11l
    └─ To reduce: Change to 5 or 10

2️⃣  'patience': 50
    ├─ Early stopping after 50 epochs without improvement
    ├─ Stops training automatically if validation plateaus
    ├─ Best model always saved regardless
    └─ Saves GPU time and energy

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✨ Save Every Epoch:
   After epoch 1  → weights/epoch1.pt saved
   After epoch 2  → weights/epoch2.pt saved
   After epoch 50 → weights/epoch50.pt saved
   ...
   After epoch 150 → weights/epoch150.pt saved

✨ Complete Training History:
   150 checkpoint files for full history

✨ Resume Training:

HOW TO USE IT:

Standard Training:
  python train.py

View results after training:
  • Open results.png to see all 8 training curves
  • Open results.csv in Excel to see exact metrics
  • Check weights/best.pt for inference

Resume if interrupted:
  from ultralytics import YOLO
  model = YOLO('xbd_yolo/runs/xbd_seg_v1/weights/last.pt')
  model.train(data='xbd_yolo/xbd.yaml', resume=True)

Use specific epoch:
  from ultralytics import YOLO
  model = YOLO('xbd_yolo/runs/xbd_seg_v1/weights/epoch50.pt')
  results = model.predict('image.jpg')
